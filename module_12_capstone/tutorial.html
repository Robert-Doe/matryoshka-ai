The rubric's hidden message

Evaluation (25) + Ethics (15) + Communication (15) = 55 of 100 points; raw modeling is only 15. Anyone can call .fit(). The scarce skills are measuring honestly, thinking about harm, and explaining clearly. Optimize for those. Read evaluation_rubric.md before you start.

4 · The trap to avoid

Don't spend 90% of your time chasing +2% accuracy and 10% on everything else. A simple model with a clear baseline, an honest evaluation, a real ethics section, and a followable write-up demonstrates mastery of the whole discipline — which is the point.

5 · Definition of done

✅ A reviewer can…

  • read writeup.md and understand the problem and why it matters;
  • run your code and reproduce the results;
  • see the right metric, a baseline, and where the model fails;
  • find an ethics/limitations section that takes harm seriously;
  • learn what you'd do next.

6 · Finish strong

Fill writeup_template.md as you go, and before you call it done, run your own model through ../module_11_ethics/red_team_checklist.md. Then look back: you built ML from scratch, trained neural nets, wielded LLMs, and learned to audit for harm. Go build something you're proud of.
